# Product

## Register

product

## Users
EduAgent serves students learning data structures and algorithms, especially learners who need an adaptive path rather than a static course outline. They use the product during focused study sessions where they need to understand a concept, practice it, ask follow-up questions, and see whether they are ready to move forward.

## Product Purpose
EduAgent is a multi-agent adaptive learning workspace. It combines profiling, path planning, resource generation, tutoring, validation, and assessment so each learner can move through a course with targeted resources and feedback. Success means the learner can see the next useful action quickly, trust the system's diagnosis, and stay oriented as resources and recommendations update.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, encouraging. The product should feel like a serious learning instrument with a helpful tutor beside it: technical enough to earn trust, restrained enough for long sessions, and warm enough that students are willing to keep asking questions.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic AI-dashboard spectacle: glowing glass panels, decorative gradients, oversized hero metrics, side-stripe card accents, and motion that competes with the task. Avoid classroom-toy visuals that make the system feel less rigorous, and avoid dense enterprise dashboards that hide the learner's next step.

## Design Principles
- Keep the next learning action visible.
- Make system intelligence legible, not theatrical.
- Prefer stable product patterns over decorative novelty.
- Separate learning content, tutoring, path state, and assessment without fragmenting the workflow.
- Support long study sessions with high contrast, predictable controls, and reduced visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Aim for WCAG AA contrast for all text and controls. Preserve keyboard focus visibility, touch targets of at least 44px on mobile, reduced-motion behavior for nonessential animation, and user controls for higher contrast and readable font sizing.
